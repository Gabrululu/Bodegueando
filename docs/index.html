Architecture

Get paid, earn points and extend credit,
with Arbitrum under the hood.

Bodegueando digitizes the Peruvian corner store (bodega) without asking it to change how it gets paid. On top of every sale in soles, an on-chain layer on Arbitrum settles the payment in USDG, mints cashback and builds the financial history this segment has never had — all deployed, verified and tested with real transactions on Arbitrum Sepolia, not in a simulated environment.

Mechanism

From a phone payment to on-chain cashback

Neither the shopkeeper nor the customer ever sees "gas", "wallet" or an address. Under the hood, every payment is a sponsored UserOperation (ERC-4337) that a contract routes in USDG, mints points for, records in the credit history and announces on Telegram — all with a single signature.

On-chain — Arbitrum Sepolia

Eleven contracts, each with one specific job

All verified — Solidity with readable source on Arbiscan, FiadoScoring with a reproducible build via cargo stylus verify. Everything that moves money settles in Paxos USDG.

Off-chain

What doesn't need to be trustless

Location, 6-digit codes and Telegram profiles are UX metadata — they live in Redis, not in a contract, on purpose.

Login
Privy (phone/email/passkey) — creates an invisible embedded wallet on first login, never "connect your wallet".
AI oracle
Claude reads the on-chain history and proposes a score/limit explained in plain Spanish — bounded by FiadoScoring's circuit breaker.
Telegram bot
Webhook in production (no polling — Vercel doesn't run daemons); sends payment alerts and answers /perfil, always in soles.
Bodega map
MapLibre + CARTO tiles — each bodega saves its location and customers pay straight from the pin.
Storage
Upstash Redis in production, with an automatic local-file fallback in development.
Exchange rate
USD → PEN, read-only and cached — users type and see soles; the app signs the USDG equivalent. Nobody sees "USDG" or "ETH".

Business model

Getting paid stays free for the bodega

No revenue comes out of the shopkeeper's pocket for their daily sales — all four mechanisms charge whoever receives real new value, and none is switched on yet: infrastructure and trust first, monetization second.

Banks / microlenders

Pay to query the credit certificate — the same model as a traditional credit bureau.

Bodegas, optional

A small spread on CreditLine interest, only if they choose to borrow.

Distributors

A brokerage fee on the demand already aggregated in GroupOrders.

Social programs

An administration fee on BeneficioToken, far below today's leakage.

Mechanism 1 (certificate queries) is the first one that can be switched on — it doesn't depend on deep liquidity or an external agreement, only on bodegas with real certificates. Until then, sponsored gas and the future on/off-ramp are operating costs, not revenue.
